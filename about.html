The short version

Curiosity first, then the build.

I'm Abdul Ahad Ikkeri — a third-year Computer Science Engineering student at PA College of Engineering.

I went to school at IIS Jubail in Saudi Arabia, finishing Class 10 with a CGPA of 8.2 and Class 12 (PCMCS) with 8.0. Those years taught me to work steadily. Engineering is teaching me to ship.

Since then I've been building in public. CommitIt is a TypeScript Git notifier that messages you instead of emailing, grouping commits by author and by what they did. On Syncura, an AI-automated healthcare ICU model system, I contributed the website front end. I also built a drone from scratch — because hardware humbles you quickly.

Outside code, I organise. I led the 30 Days Coding Challenge, ran events for IEEE Week 2026, handled registration for EmbedThon, and taught a two-hour beginner Gen-AI workshop. Teaching is the fastest way I know to find the holes in my own understanding.
